The shares will list on the BSE SME platform. This guide covers the price, GMP, key dates and allotment details.

## About Sollfege Smart Electronics

The company was incorporated in Kolkata in 2012 as Denn Audio Private Limited. It began as a distributor of premium audio equipment and now offers audio, video, home automation and smart living products. Its range includes home theatre systems, speakers, soundbars, projectors, smart lighting, security systems and smart locks.

## Sollfege Smart Electronics IPO Price and Price Band

This is a fixed price issue, so there is no price range. The Sollfege Smart Electronics IPO price is ₹55 per share, with a face value of ₹10. The issue consists of 39,60,000 fresh shares. No shares are being sold by existing shareholders.
